// Install

Put it on your home screen

No app store, no download button. Open rift.rivi.us/play on your phone, add it to your home screen, and it installs like any other game — full screen, its own icon, works offline.

Android Open in Chrome  ·  tap ⋮  ·  “Add to Home screen” → Install Free iPhone Open in Safari  ·  tap Share  ·  “Add to Home Screen” Free
Yours, on your device: your fleet saves right on your phone — no account, no sign-up, no connection required. Once installed, Rift launches and plays even in airplane mode.

// The Game

Build a fleet. Win the galaxy.

Every system — fleets, combat, ranks, the economy — written from scratch in Rust, and the exact same rules run whether you play solo or online.

Fleet

Fleet Management

Buy, sell, repair, and field up to 20 ships drawn from 30 hulls across six tiers — from the nimble Falcon to the endgame Leviathan.

Combat

Tactical Battles

Choose Aggressive, Defensive, or Tactical formations. Shield-reduced damage, evasion rolls, and per-ship fire rate decide 1v1 duels up to 20v20 fleet clashes.

Progress

Ranks & Economy

Climb 11 ranks from Recruit to Fleet Admiral, each unlocking stat bonuses and bigger fleets. Earn credits through rank-gated work between battles.

Boarding

Boarding Raids

Before the fleets clash, board the enemy flagship and fight through its corridors in first person. Cripple their defenses, then finish it in open space.

// Mobile

Made for your pocket

Rift is a mobile game: the whole galaxy — fleets, battles, boarding raids, the leaderboard — runs right on your phone.

Instant

Tap the icon, you're flying

Once it's on your home screen, Rift opens full screen in landscape like a real installed game — because it is one. Pick a callsign, buy your first ship, and take on the NPC fleets that patrol the galaxy.

Eleven NPC admirals from Astro to Centauri stand between you and the top of the leaderboard.

Offline

No signal? No problem

Everything is saved on your device, and the game itself is cached the first time you open it. Subway, airplane, basement — your fleet is always ready to fight.

Your save never leaves your phone: no account, no tracking, no ads.
