How it works

Under the hood is a ResNet-50 we fine-tuned in-house with transfer learning: the ImageNet backbone is mostly frozen, and the last stage plus a new classification head are trained on pet photos. It recognizes the 37 breeds (25 dog, 12 cat) in the Oxford-IIIT Pet dataset and returns its top five guesses with confidence scores. Your photos are processed in memory and never stored.
