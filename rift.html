🚀 Rift

A spaceship fleet-battle game built in pure Rust — for your phone. Buy and refit warships, build a fleet, pick a tactical formation, climb 11 military ranks, and battle NPC admirals for the top of the leaderboard. Installs on Android and iPhone straight from the browser.

Features

  • Fleet management — buy, sell, repair, and field up to 20 ships drawn from a 30-hull catalog across six tiers.
  • Tactical battles — choose Aggressive, Defensive, or Tactical formations; shields, evasion rolls, and per-ship fire rate decide everything from 1v1 duels to 20v20 fleet clashes.
  • Ranks & economy — climb 11 military ranks from Recruit to Fleet Admiral, each unlocking stat bonuses and bigger fleets; earn credits through rank-gated work between battles.
  • Boarding raids — board the enemy flagship before the fleets clash and fight through its corridors in first-person 3D.
  • Plays offline — your fleet saves right on your phone — no account, no connection required, even in airplane mode.
  • Pure Rust, on your phone — every system — simulation, rendering, UI — written from scratch and compiled to WebAssembly; installs on Android and iPhone straight from the browser.
