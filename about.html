About the studio

Kestrel Labs is an independent software studio that builds products end to end — training the models, writing the servers, and shipping the pages you're looking at.

Our current products are AniTrans, a pet breed detector powered by a ResNet-50 fine-tuned in-house, and Rift, a multiplayer spaceship fleet-battle game written in pure Rust — from the combat simulation to the networking to the UI.
