🔮 AniTrans

Snap a photo of your cat or dog and we'll read their mood, tell you what they probably want — and guess the breed while we're at it.

Snap a photo of your pet

Reading their mind…

How it works

A ResNet-50 fine-tuned in-house on ~5,600 labeled photos of pet expressions reads the mood — happy, relaxed, sad, or angry — and translates it into what your pet probably wants. A second model guesses the breed. Your photos are processed in memory and never stored.
